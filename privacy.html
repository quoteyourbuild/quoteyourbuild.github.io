Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains how QuoteYourBuild Apps Pty Ltd (ABN 60 702 391 599) ("we", "us") handles personal information in connection with the QuoteYourBuild web and iPhone app and this website. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Information we collect

  • Account details you give us, such as your name, business name and email address.
  • Job and quote content you enter into the app, such as measurements, material quantities, costs, and customer details you choose to include on quotes.
  • Subscription and billing information needed to manage your subscription. Payments are processed by our payment provider or by Apple through the App Store; we do not store full card details.
  • Messages you send us, for example by email.

How we use it

We use this information to provide and support the app, manage your account and subscription, respond to enquiries, keep the service secure, and improve the product. We do not sell your personal information.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run the app (such as hosting and payment providers), when you ask us to (for example, when you share a quote with your customer), or when required by law.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. Some of our service providers may store data outside Australia.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or close your account, by emailing hello@quoteyourbuild.com.

Contact and complaints

For privacy questions or complaints, email hello@quoteyourbuild.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes
